There is no single answer to what a website costs in Pakistan, because two quotes for "a five-page website" are rarely for the same thing. One may be a template with your logo dropped in. The other may include a custom design, written copy, speed work, a contact system that actually reaches you, and a year of updates. That is why the website cost in Pakistan varies so much from one quote to the next: the scope changes, not the page count.

The useful way to think about it is in two parts. There is the **build**, which you pay once, and there are the **running costs** (domain, hosting, licences, maintenance), which you pay every year for as long as the site is live. This guide explains what drives each part, gives the running-cost figures we could confirm from official price lists, and shows how to compare quotes like for like so a cheap one doesn't turn into an expensive one.

## Why website development prices in Pakistan vary so much

Most articles on this topic list price bands such as "basic site: Rs X to Rs Y". Those bands are opinions, not market data, and they hide the real question: what exactly is being built?

A quote is a bundle of decisions: who designs the site, who writes the content, what it has to do beyond showing information, how carefully it is built and tested, and what happens after launch. Two developers can make opposite choices on every one of these and still call the result "a five-page website". The cheapest quotes usually save money on the parts you can't see in a screenshot, such as speed on a mid-range phone, tested forms, backups and updates.

## Types of website and what each one involves

Page count is a poor measure. A better one is how many *different kinds* of page and feature the site needs.

| Type of site | Typical purpose | What usually drives the cost |
|---|---|---|
| Single landing page | One offer, one call to action, often for ads | Copy, design quality, form or WhatsApp hand-off, tracking setup |
| Business or marketing site | Explain services, build trust, get enquiries | Number of page layouts, custom design, content writing, local search setup |
| Lead-generation site | Quote or estimate requests, bookings | Form logic, where enquiries go (email, WhatsApp, CRM), spam protection |
| Online store | Sell products with delivery | Catalogue size, variants, payment methods, delivery rules, stock and order handling |
| Custom web app or portal | Logins, dashboards, live data | Every feature is built and tested; closer to software than to a website |

For example, the [QILM Institute](https://qilminstitute.com) site we built looks like a normal marketing site, but its free-trial booking detects the visitor's time zone and it has separate pages for UK, US and Gulf time zones. If your project is closer to the last row, it is priced and planned more like software than a website. ## What drives website development charges

When you ask for quotes, these are the line items that should be visible, even if the developer bundles them into one figure:

1. **Discovery and a written brief.** Someone has to turn "we need a website" into a list of pages, features and integrations. Without this, every quote is a guess.
2. **Design.** Template customisation, or original layouts for each type of page, built around your content.
3. **Build.** Turning the design into working pages, including mobile layouts.
4. **Content.** Copywriting, translation (an Urdu version as well as English is a second set of content to write, check and keep up to date), photography, product data entry.
5. **Features and integrations.** Payment gateways, booking, WhatsApp, email marketing, analytics, chat assistants. Each one is setup plus testing. If you are thinking of adding an assistant, our post on [adding an AI chatbot to your website](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-your-website/) explains what is involved.
6. **Speed and search basics.** Image compression, clean page titles, a sitemap, Search Console setup. Our guide to [SEO for small businesses](/blog/seo-for-small-businesses/) covers what this should include.
7. **Migration.** Moving content, products or orders from an old site, and redirecting old links so you don't lose search traffic.
8. **Training and handover.** Showing your team how to edit the site, and giving you every login.
9. **Support after launch.** Fixes, updates and backups for an agreed period.

## WordPress vs custom website cost

The WordPress software itself is free. It is [licensed under the GPL and can be used for commercial projects without licensing fees](https://wordpress.org/about/features/). What you pay for is the work around it, plus any paid themes and plugins.

Those paid add-ons are usually annual. For example, Elementor's [pricing page](https://elementor.com/pricing/) listed its Essential plan for one website at $60 for the first year in November 2026, charged annually. Many WordPress sites run several paid licences like this, and each one needs renewing or it stops getting updates.

A custom-coded site (for example, built with a framework such as Next.js) has no theme or page-builder licences, but every feature is written rather than installed, so the build usually takes longer. Hosting can be cheap: Netlify's [pricing page](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/) listed a free plan with 300 credits a month and a Personal plan at $9 a month in November 2026, which suits many small, fast sites.

A practical decision rule:

- **Choose WordPress** if your team will edit content often, you need a common feature that already exists as a well-maintained plugin, and you accept the ongoing update work.
- **Choose custom** if the site has logic no plugin does well, speed matters a lot, or you want fewer moving parts to keep patched.

Either is reasonable, as long as the quote says which one you are getting and what it costs to run.

## E-commerce website cost in Pakistan

An online store costs more than a brochure site because there is more to get right: product variants, delivery charges by city, cash on delivery, stock, order emails, returns. The platform choice also shapes your running costs.

- **WooCommerce** (on WordPress) is a [free plugin, and WooCommerce adds no transaction fee beyond your payment provider's](https://woocommerce.com/wordpress-ecommerce/). Paid extensions are sold as annual subscriptions. WooCommerce includes cash on delivery and free delivery above a minimum order amount as standard options. [Mian Art Store](https://mianartstore.com), which we built on WooCommerce, uses both: cash on delivery, and free delivery on orders over Rs. 3,000.
- **Shopify** charges a monthly subscription. Its [pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) listed the Basic plan at $29 a month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly, in November 2026. Shopify Payments is only available in [the countries Shopify lists](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries), and Pakistan was not on that list at the time of writing, so a Pakistani store would use a third-party gateway. Shopify's pricing page lists a third-party transaction fee of 2% on the Basic plan in that case, on top of the gateway's own fee.
- **Payment gateway fees** apply on any platform. Safepay's [published pricing](https://getsafepay.pk/pricing), for example, listed 2.9% + Rs 30 per successful domestic card payment, with no setup or monthly fee, when we checked in November 2026. On [shapargullay](https://shapargullay.com), the ticketing platform we built on Safepay, the 4% service fee is shown to the buyer before they pay, so nobody is surprised at checkout.

If you are selling online for the first time, our guide to [starting an online store in Pakistan](/blog/start-an-online-store-in-pakistan/) goes through payments, delivery and cash on delivery in more detail.

## Recurring costs: domain, hosting and maintenance

This is the part most quotes leave out, and it is the part you pay every year. Figures below are from each provider's own published prices, checked in November 2026. Prices change, so confirm before you buy.

| Cost | Example figure | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|
| .pk domain | Rs 2,100 a year for Pakistan-based registrants, paid every two years (Rs 4,200) | [PKNIC](https://www.pknic.net.pk/), the .pk registry, raised the fee from Rs 1,800 on 1 August 2026. Resellers may add a margin. |
| Shared hosting | Rs 599 a month on a 48-month term (Rs 28,752 upfront), renewing at Rs 1,699 a month, excluding taxes | Hostinger's [Pakistan pricing](https://www.hostinger.com/pk/web-hosting) for its Premium plan. Note the gap between first-term and renewal price. |
| Static or modern-framework hosting | Free plan, or $9 a month | [Netlify pricing](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/). Suits fast sites without a database. |
| SSL certificate (the padlock) | Free | [Let's Encrypt](https://letsencrypt.org/) issues free certificates, and most hosts install them automatically. |
| Theme and plugin licences | $60 a year for one Elementor Essential licence, as one example | [Elementor pricing](https://elementor.com/pricing/). Count every paid add-on in your quote. |
| Business email | Priced per user, per month | [Google Workspace pricing](https://workspace.google.com/pricing) varies by region and billing plan. |
| Maintenance | Agreed with your developer | Updates, backups, uptime checks and small fixes. See below. |

Note how hosting is sold: an introductory rate on a long term, with renewal close to three times as much per month in the example above. Always ask for the renewal price. Dollar-priced services also move with the exchange rate.

### Why maintenance is not optional

A website is software, and software needs patching. Patchstack, a WordPress security company, reported in its [State of WordPress Security in 2026](https://patchstack.com/whitepaper/state-of-wordpress-security-in-2026/) that 11,334 new vulnerabilities were found in the WordPress ecosystem in 2025, 91% of them in plugins, and that 46% had no fix from the developer by the time they were disclosed. A site nobody updates is an easy target. Whoever you hire, agree who applies updates, how often backups run and where they are stored.

Send every developer the same written brief, then put their answers side by side.

| Question | Quote A | Quote B | Quote C |

| Mobile and speed testing included? | | | |
| Revisions included, and cost of extra changes | | | |
| Payment milestones | | | |
| Support period after launch, and what it covers | | | |
| Monthly or yearly running cost after year one | | | |

Then add up the **first-year total** (build plus a year of running costs) and the **three-year total**. A quote that is cheaper to build can easily cost more by year three if it relies on several paid plugins, expensive hosting renewals, or a developer who charges for every small change. ## Red flags in cheap website quotes

Cheap is not automatically bad: a simple site on a good template, with your own content, can be done well for little money. These are the signs a low price is low for the wrong reasons:

- **No written scope.** Just "5-page website, Rs X". You can't hold anyone to that.
- **Domain and hosting "included free".** Ask whose name they are in. If the developer registers your domain in their name, you don't control your own address.
- **Nulled or "lifetime" premium themes and plugins.** Pirated copies get no security updates and sometimes contain malware. Ask for proof of licences in your name.
- **Guaranteed Google rankings.** Google itself says [no one can guarantee a #1 ranking](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo).
- **Full payment upfront.** Reasonable developers ask for a deposit and tie the rest to milestones you can see.
- **No staging link.** You only see the site on launch day, when changes are hardest.
- **No mention of updates or backups.** The quote ends at launch, and so does the developer's interest.
- **Unclear handover.** No commitment to give you admin logins, files and source code at the end.

## Questions

### What is the cheapest way to get a website in Pakistan?

Use a well-maintained template or a hosted builder, write your own text, supply your own photos, and keep features to a contact form and WhatsApp link. You will still pay for a domain and hosting each year. Make sure the domain is registered in your name.

### How much does a .pk domain cost?

PKNIC's published fee for Pakistan-based registrants is Rs 2,100 a year from 1 August 2026, charged on a two-year basis (Rs 4,200 for two years). Resellers may charge more. Check [PKNIC](https://www.pknic.net.pk/) before paying.

### Is WordPress cheaper than a custom website?

Usually to build, not always to run. WordPress is free, but paid themes and plugins renew yearly and need regular updates. A custom site costs more upfront but can have lower running costs. Compare three-year totals, not build prices.

### Should I pay monthly for a website instead?

Some agencies offer a monthly fee that covers build, hosting and support. It can work, but check who owns the domain, design and code if you stop paying. If the answer is "we do", you are renting, not buying.
